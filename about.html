About PastureConnect

Connecting pastoral communities with shared resource information.

Our Mission

To make information about pasture and water conditions easier for pastoral communities to share and access.

Our Vision

Communities using accessible local information to make better-informed livestock management decisions.

Our Values

Community cooperation, responsible reporting, sustainability, and access to information.

The problem we address

During dry seasons, finding pasture and water can be difficult. Livestock keepers may not know the conditions in other areas before travelling.

Our proposed solution

PastureConnect provides a platform where people can submit and view reports about pasture and water conditions. The information can support planning, although users should verify conditions before travelling.

PastureConnect — Healthy pastures. Stronger communities.
